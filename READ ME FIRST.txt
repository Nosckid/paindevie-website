PAIN DE VIE — WEBSITE
=====================

WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html   -> the website itself. You never need to open or edit this.
  Images/      -> every picture the site uses, sorted into folders.

HOW TO CHANGE A PICTURE (no code, no editing)
  1. Open the Images folder and go to the picture you want to change.
     Example: Images / Poultry / Whole wing (3-joint)
  2. Delete the file that is in there.
  3. Put your own picture in that same folder.
  4. Rename your picture to:  photo
     (so it becomes photo.jpg, or photo.png — whatever type it already is)
  5. Save, then re-upload the whole folder to your website host.

  Accepted picture types: .jpg  .jpeg  .png  .webp
  The file MUST be named "photo", in small letters, and the extension
  must be in small letters too (photo.jpg, NOT Photo.JPG).
  That is how the site finds it.

THE DIRECTOR'S PHOTO
  Folder:  Images / Director
  It is empty right now, and the website shows a grey placeholder.
  Put the director's photo in that folder, name it "photo.jpg",
  and it appears automatically. The orange "add here" note disappears
  on its own once the photo is in place.
  A portrait-shaped photo (taller than wide) looks best.

SUPPLIER LOGOS
  Folder:  Images / Suppliers
  Each numbered folder holds one supplier logo.
    01 = Capago            05 = Tomex
    02 = Mondial Foods     06 = Polar Fresh Group
    03 = Saria             07 = Al Khaleej
    04 = Seafoodia         08 = Brittania
  Folders 09 to 14 are empty and ready for new suppliers.
  Drop a logo into any empty one (named "photo.jpg") and it joins the
  scrolling strip by itself. Remove a logo file and that supplier
  disappears from the strip. Logos on a white background look best.

THE COMPANY LOGO
  Folder:  Images / Logo
  Keep the name exactly "logo.png" if you ever replace it, and keep a
  see-through (transparent) background.

THE CONTACT FORM  (one setting to switch on, once)
  When a visitor fills the form and clicks "Send inquiry", the message
  goes to Netlify, and Netlify emails it to you. You must switch the
  email on once:
    1. Deploy the site to Netlify.
    2. In Netlify open your site -> Forms -> Form notifications.
    3. Add notification -> Email notification.
    4. Put paindevie2018@gmail.com as the address, choose the form
       named "contact", and save.
  From then on every inquiry arrives in that inbox. The subject line
  the visitor typed becomes the email subject. You can also read all
  submissions any time under Forms in Netlify.
  Free plan allows 100 form submissions per month.

  After sending, the visitor sees the thank-you.html page. Keep that
  file in the folder.

YOUR WEB ADDRESS
  The site is set up for:  https://pain2vie.com
  If that ever changes, tell me and I will update the address inside
  index.html, sitemap.xml and robots.txt.

PUTTING THE SITE ONLINE
  Upload the WHOLE folder to your host. On Netlify: drag this entire
  folder onto the deploy area. Keep every file side by side - do not
  separate index.html from the Images folder.

  The folder also contains, and these should stay where they are:
    thank-you.html  - the page shown after the form is sent
    sitemap.xml     - helps Google list your site
    robots.txt      - tells search engines they may list your site

TIP
  After re-uploading, press Ctrl+Shift+R (or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac)
  to make your browser show the new pictures instead of the old ones.
