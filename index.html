From source to table

Our quality, sourcing and import process

We control every link in the chain, so what leaves the origin arrives exactly as intended.

1

Sourcing

We select trusted suppliers and premium catches and harvests at origin.

2

Import

Goods move under refrigeration, cleared and documented for wholesale.

3

Cold storage

Held at controlled temperature in our facility until dispatch.

4

Delivery

Refrigerated transport brings full cartons to your door, cold intact.
